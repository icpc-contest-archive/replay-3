Foreign Teams Registration - Result

2026-10-24

ICPC Korea Committee has decided to accept the first 7 eligible foreign teams in the registration queue as follows.

  • Tideology (Natl Taiwan U, Taiwan)
  • Sendy2026 (Senshu U, Japan)
  • Phantasm (Institute of Science Tokyo, Japan)
  • - -* (Chulalongkorn U, Thailand)
  • NYCU_FriedRice (NYCU, Taiwan)
  • u_u ..zZZ (U Tokyo, Japan)
  • Graduation party! (Natl U Singapore, Singapore)

Since a total of 9 teams have already registered, we have now closed the registration.

Should an accepted team ranked higher in the selection process decline to participate, the next team in the queue will be invited.

Teams that have been accepted are advised to monitor future announcements closely, and ensure they have all the necessary documents prepared for on-site registration (e.g. a hard copy of their certificate of enrollment).

If any of your team member requires an official invitation letter, please let us know by providing the necessary information for the letter through your team coach.
